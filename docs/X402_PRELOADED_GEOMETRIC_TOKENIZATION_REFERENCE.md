# x402 Preloaded Geometric Tokenization Reference

This document adds a structured reference for the uploaded paper **"Preloaded Geometric Tokenization"**.

## Added artifact

- `docs/x402-preloaded-geometric-tokenization.json`

## What it contains

The JSON extraction currently captures the visible portions of the provided text, including:

- the preloaded-unity framing of tokenization
- the count vs depth mechanics
- the Master Table as fixed preloaded vocabulary
- the closure-generated address/value relationships
- the birth of the binary sequence from `count 1 of 1 unit -> 2 of 1`
- the beginning of compounding operations
- the v0.3 idea of indefinite inverse counting beyond count 5
- on-demand refinement by continuing the same bitstream
- the overlay-layer positioning for physical data

## Important note

This is a **structured extraction**, not yet a full canonical transcription of the entire paper.

The visible excerpt includes the introductory framing, contents, and early sections, but not the full body of Sections 4–13 or the appendices. Because of that, the JSON file explicitly marks itself as a partial extraction.
