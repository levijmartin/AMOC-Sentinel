# Future Caribbean Buildathon Notes

## What it is

Future Caribbean launched a global Agentic AI buildathon focused on building practical systems with Caribbean relevance and real-world commercial potential.

## Key details

- 40 teams selected
- applications close July 3, 2026
- build sprint runs July 17 to August 7, 2026
- winners announced September 1, 2026
- teams get access to high-end compute, mentorship, and advisors
- teams keep their IP
- investor showcase follows for strongest teams

## Stated focus

The event is centered on practical Agentic AI systems for Caribbean opportunity areas such as:

- finance
- healthcare
- tourism
- food systems
- climate risk
- disaster coordination
- blue economy
- open track for broader AI systems and tools

## What this means for the repo

A strong submission should likely include:

- a clear regional or industry problem
- an agentic AI workflow, not just a simple chatbot
- a believable deployment path
- measurable business or public-value impact
- a demoable MVP within 21 days

## Strong project pattern for this buildathon

Best fit ideas probably look like:

1. painful real problem
2. clear user
3. agentic workflow with actions, tools, or automation
4. Caribbean-specific angle or advantage
5. realistic MVP and investor story

## Good deliverables to prepare

- application summary
- one-paragraph pitch
- architecture diagram
- feature list for MVP
- demo script
- deployment plan
- team roles and timeline
