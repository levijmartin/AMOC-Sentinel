# Opportunity Prioritization

## Scoring criteria

Each opportunity should be judged against:

- Caribbean relevance
- quality and availability of public data
- operational usefulness
- agentic AI fit
- MVP complexity for 21 days
- commercial upside
- deployability with low regulatory friction

## Ranked opportunities

### 1. Coastal Risk & Early Warning
**Why it ranks high:**
- strongest fit with current repo direction
- obvious Caribbean relevance
- strong public-value and business-value story
- good fit for threshold alerts, forecasting, and action planning
- usable with current planned sources such as Copernicus, RAPID, CariCOOS, and NDBC

**Buildathon strength:** very high

### 2. Sargassum Prediction, Response & Utilization
**Why it ranks high:**
- highly Caribbean-specific
- operationally visible and easy to demo
- connects forecasting, coordination, logistics, and utilization
- strong public and commercial narrative

**Buildathon strength:** very high

### 3. Open Maritime Logistics Layer
**Why it ranks high:**
- strong commercial potential
- real operator pain point
- naturally real-time and decision-oriented
- excellent long-term platform potential

**Buildathon strength:** high

### 4. Maritime & Vessel Intelligence
**Why it ranks high:**
- rich data possibilities
- strong operator use cases
- real-time outputs are natural
- anomaly and route-risk features fit agentic workflows well

**Buildathon strength:** high

### 5. Fisheries Intelligence
**Why it ranks high:**
- practical and relevant
- useful data connections through ocean conditions
- good operator-facing value

**Buildathon strength:** medium-high

### 6. Ocean Data Infrastructure
**Why it ranks lower for first build:**
- strategically valuable, but more infrastructure-heavy
- risk of building plumbing instead of a compelling user-facing product

**Buildathon strength:** medium

### 7. Ridge-to-Reef Systems
**Why it ranks lower for first build:**
- scientifically valuable but harder to deliver in a clean 21-day MVP
- potentially broader data integration burden

**Buildathon strength:** medium

### 8. Ocean Energy & Weather Intelligence
**Why it ranks lower for first build:**
- interesting and useful, but often requires more domain-specific integration
- may be harder to demonstrate clearly in a short sprint

**Buildathon strength:** medium

## Recommended primary direction

### Current recommendation: Coastal Risk & Early Warning
This remains the best fit for the existing StormShield Caribbean direction because it combines:
- clear user value
- strong Caribbean relevance
- actionability
- compatible data sources already identified in the repo
- good narrative for judges and future customers

## Recommended secondary directions

If the project pivots, the strongest alternatives are:
- Sargassum Prediction, Response & Utilization
- Open Maritime Logistics Layer

## Practical decision rule

If the goal is the strongest buildathon entry, choose the concept that:
- can be demonstrated with live or recent data
- produces operator-facing actions, not just analysis
- can be explained in one sentence
- has a believable path to deployment after the sprint
