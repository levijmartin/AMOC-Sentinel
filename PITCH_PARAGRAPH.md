# Buildathon Pitch Paragraph

StormShield Caribbean is an agentic climate-risk intelligence platform designed to help Caribbean property owners and small businesses move from climate anxiety to practical resilience planning. The system combines high-quality open data sources including RAPID AMOC measurements, Copernicus Marine ocean products, and Caribbean observational feeds such as CariCOOS and NOAA NDBC to detect meaningful environmental risk signals and translate them into localized, actionable guidance. Instead of functioning like a simple weather dashboard or chatbot, StormShield Caribbean uses an agent-based workflow to gather signals, interpret climate and ocean risk, evaluate local vulnerability, and generate phased resilience action plans. The result is a tool that helps users understand what matters most, what to do first, and how to strengthen business and property readiness before the next major storm event.
