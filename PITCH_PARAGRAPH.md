# Buildathon Pitch Paragraph

AMOC Sentinel is a cold blob-to-Caribbean risk operating system designed to turn ocean observations into trusted intelligence and economic value for Caribbean resilience. The system combines public ocean, weather, and observational data sources such as RAPID, Copernicus Marine, CariCOOS, and NOAA NDBC to track ocean and weather shifts, verify data quality and provenance, and transform trusted signals into actionable risk intelligence. Instead of acting like a passive dashboard, AMOC Sentinel uses an agent-based workflow to ingest and validate environmental context, interpret Atlantic and Caribbean risk conditions, assess local vulnerability, and generate action briefs, alerts, and resilience plans operators can actually use. Over time, the platform can evolve into a hybrid access model with a free public-interest layer and a machine-payable premium intelligence layer, where x402-style payment rails enable per-report, per-alert, or per-query access for operators, developers, researchers, and partners.
