# Buildathon Pitch Paragraph

StormShield Caribbean is an agentic climate-risk intelligence platform designed to help Caribbean property owners and small businesses move from climate anxiety to practical resilience planning. The system combines public ocean, weather, and observational data sources such as RAPID, Copernicus Marine, CariCOOS, and NOAA NDBC with user-specific exposure details to identify meaningful storm, flood, outage, and coastal risk signals. Instead of acting like a passive dashboard, StormShield Caribbean uses an agent-based workflow to gather environmental context, interpret Atlantic and Caribbean risk conditions, assess local vulnerability, and generate phased resilience action plans that operators can actually use. The result is a tool that helps users understand what matters most, what to do first, and how to strengthen property and business readiness before the next major storm event. Over time, the platform can also evolve into a machine-payable climate-risk API and premium alerting system, where x402-style payment rails enable per-report, per-alert, or per-query access for operators, developers, and partners.
