System architecture

Three layers of resilient sensing

The concept separates sensing, fusion, and coordination so each claim can be prototyped and tested independently.

01 / SENSE

Bio-inspired magnetoreception

A proposed engineered sensing layer would translate local magnetic-field-dependent reactions into measurable signals.

  • Local environmental input
  • No GPS coordinate dependency
  • Requires laboratory validation
02 / FUSE

Hawk-Vision edge fusion

Magnetic heading is compared with visual odometry and inertial measurements rather than trusted as a single source of truth.

  • Cross-sensor agreement checks
  • Drift and anomaly scoring
  • Local decision support
03 / COORDINATE

Peer state correlation

Multiple platforms could exchange signed state summaries to compare confidence without describing the link as quantum entanglement.

  • Conventional data links
  • Correlated observations
  • Fault-tolerant consensus research

Evidence roadmap

What must be proven next

  1. Sensor feasibilityDemonstrate repeatable magnetic-field response, sensitivity, and environmental stability.
  2. Fusion benchmarkCompare navigation error against established visual-inertial and magnetic methods.
  3. Adversarial testingMeasure behavior under interference, drift, occlusion, and conflicting sensors.
  4. Hardware integrationEstablish manufacturability, power budget, calibration, and lifecycle performance.
